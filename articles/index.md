# Articles

### Guides

- [Metric
  overview](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/metrics-overview.md):

  Choose a metric from the relationship your embedding should preserve.

- [Local
  preservation](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/local-preservation.md):

  Choose and interpret metrics for neighbor identity, rank, local scale,
  graph structure, and multiple neighborhood sizes.

- [Global
  preservation](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/global-preservation.md):

  Choose and interpret sampled metrics for global distance order,
  association, distributions, and matched magnitudes.

- [Label
  retrieval](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/label-retrieval.md):

  Choose ROC or precision-recall AUC and inspect overall, per-label, and
  per-query retrieval scores.
