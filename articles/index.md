# Articles

### Guides

- [Metric
  overview](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/metrics-overview.md):

  Choose a metric from the relationship your embedding should preserve.

- [Local
  preservation](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/local-preservation.md):

  Choose and interpret metrics for neighbor identity, rank, local scale,
  graph structure, and multiple neighborhood sizes.

- [Global
  preservation](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/global-preservation.md):

  Choose and interpret sampled metrics for global distance order,
  association, distributions, and matched magnitudes.

- [Label
  retrieval](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/label-retrieval.md):

  Choose ROC or precision-recall AUC and inspect overall, per-label, and
  per-query retrieval scores.

### Workflows

- [Evaluating PCA, UMAP and t-SNE on
  MNIST](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/mnist-pca-umap.md):

  Compare neighborhood, distance, and label preservation on the same
  digits.

- [When two digits set the scale of an LTSA
  map](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/ltsa-map-spread.md):

  Compare neighbor overlap and fitted-scale stress on MNIST, then change
  the reference on a Swiss roll.

- [Which distances should a Swiss roll
  preserve?](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/reference-geometry.md):

  Evaluate PCA and LTSA against rolled and intrinsic geometry.

- [Distance fidelity across
  scales](https://jlmelville.github.io/quadra/articles/distance-band-fidelity.md):

  Compare distance order and magnitude from nearby input pairs to the
  whole dataset.
